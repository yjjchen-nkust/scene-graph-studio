import { Formal, Implications, Intuition, Worked } from './math/Parts';

<Step id="s1">

## A third element in the signature

A video scene graph is not an image scene graph computed repeatedly. Its signature is
$G = (V, E, T)$, and the third element is not decoration: an edge has a birth and a death, and the
question of whether an edge that appears in one frame and vanishes in the next is a real event or
detector noise has no counterpart in the still-image problem.

Everything in this module follows from taking that question seriously.

</Step>

<Step id="s2">

<Intuition>

An edge is present at a timestamp if it is alive then and its confidence clears a threshold. Raise
the threshold and fewer edges survive at every timestamp — so the threshold is not a detail of
post-processing, it is the dial that trades temporal recall against stability.

</Intuition>

<Formal>

$$
G=(V,E,T),\qquad E=\bigcup_{t\in T}E_t,\qquad e\in E_t\iff \mathrm{born}(e)\le t\le\mathrm{die}(e)\ \wedge\ w_e\ge\theta
$$

</Formal>

<Worked>

$$
\begin{aligned}E_t &= \bigl\{e : \mathrm{born}(e)\le t\le\mathrm{die}(e)\ \wedge\ w_e\ge\theta\bigr\}\\E &= \textstyle\bigcup_{t\in T}E_t\\\theta'\ge\theta &\Rightarrow E_t(\theta')\subseteq E_t(\theta)\end{aligned}
$$

</Worked>

<Implications>

$$
\begin{gathered}\lvert E_t\rvert\text{ is non-increasing in }\theta\text{: raising the threshold trades temporal recall for stability.}\\[4pt]\text{An edge present in one frame and gone the next is either a real event or detector noise,}\\ \text{and }\theta\text{ is the only thing separating them. That is why }T\text{ belongs in the signature }G=(V,E,T)\\ \text{rather than being handled by running an image model once per frame.}\end{gathered}
$$

That is why $T$ belongs in the signature rather than being handled by running an image model once
per frame. A per-frame model has no way to express "this edge persisted", so it cannot distinguish
a flicker from an event, and $\theta$ is the only thing standing between the two.

**Action Genome** — the dataset the field uses — splits its predicates into three groups:
attention, spatial, and contacting. A pair can carry one from each simultaneously, and that is a
fact about the world rather than an annotation artefact: a person can be *looking at* and
*holding* the same cup.

This is where M4's third constraint mode earns its place. The graph constraint would force one
predicate per pair and discard two thirds of a legitimate annotation; no constraint at all admits
every runner-up. The Semi Constraint STTran proposed for Action Genome (Cong et al. 2021) keeps for each object pair
the top attention predicate and every spatial or contacting predicate scoring above 0.9. The
application's `semi` is a cap, at most two predicates per ordered object pair, which approximates
that rule without computing it.

</Implications>

</Step>

<Step id="s3">

## Five designs over the temporal axis

**STTran** is the field's baseline: a spatial encoder over each frame, a temporal decoder across
them. Its constrained-to-unconstrained gap is the figure M4 used to show how far a mode switch can
move a number.

**TEMPURA** adds memory and, more unusually, uncertainty — it models how confident it should be
rather than only what it believes, which is the closest thing in this literature to the calibration
M4 noted is absent.

**OED** removes the tracker: one stage, end to end, no separate association step. The tracker was
a large fraction of both the error and the engineering.

**DiffVsgg** and **UNO** are the recent entries, applying diffusion and unified online prediction
respectively.

Read the sequence as progressive removal of hand-built machinery — pairs in M7, the tracker here —
and notice that each removal makes the remaining failure modes easier to see rather than fewer.

</Step>

<Step id="s4">

## In the lab

Return to L2 and switch the constraint mode to `semi`. L2's fixture is not Action Genome data,
and no pair in it carries an attention and a contacting relation; its man–table pair carries two
predicted predicates, `under` and `on`. Watch what `graph` throws away and what `none` lets in:
`semi` keeps both, as it would keep an attention and a contacting relation on one pair. On Action
Genome data the middle setting is closer than either end, and it is still a cap of two per ordered
object pair, not STTran's rule.

</Step>

<Step id="s5">

## Checkpoint

Raising $\theta$ reduces $|E_t|$ at every $t$. Describe a situation in which raising it improves
the graph's usefulness, and one in which it destroys the only thing the video was recorded to
capture.

</Step>
