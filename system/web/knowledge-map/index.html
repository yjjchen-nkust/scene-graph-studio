1 · The anchor paper1 · 核心論文

Wang, Yan, Li, Liu · IndVisSGG: VLM-based scene graph generation for industrial spatial intelligence · Advanced Engineering Informatics 65 (2025) 103107Wang、Yan、Li、Liu · IndVisSGG: VLM-based scene graph generation for industrial spatial intelligence · Advanced Engineering Informatics 65 (2025) 103107

Every prior scene graph method needed annotated boxes or masks before it could predict a relation, which is why no industrial scene graph dataset existed — nobody would pay to annotate one. IndVisSGG removes the annotation step by prompting GPT-4V directly, and spends its engineering on making that prompt reliable. The whole method is three stages and four equations. 在此之前的每一個 scene graph 方法，都得先有標註好的 box 或 mask 才能預測關係——這正是工業 scene graph 資料集始終不存在的原因：沒有人願意付錢做這種標註。IndVisSGG 用直接提示 GPT-4V 的方式拿掉了標註這一步，並把工程量花在讓那個 prompt 變得可靠。整個方法就是三個階段、四條方程式。

step 1

Extract spatial–temporal triplets抽取時空三元組

Ask the model for triplets. Without criteria it answers in free text and nothing is scorable. Supplying the Triplets Extraction Criteria — a predefined object set O, predicate set P, and examples E carrying both positive and negative cases with an analysis — constrains the output to a vocabulary the metric can see. 請模型輸出三元組。沒有 criteria 時它會用自由文字回答，於是什麼都無法計分。提供 Triplets Extraction Criteria——預先定義的物件集合 O、predicate 集合 P，以及同時包含正例與反例並附上分析的範例 E——就能把輸出約束到指標看得見的詞彙裡。

step 2

Triple-check across temporal domains跨時間域的三重檢查

Run N independent experts over step one’s output. The paper names three real corrections they make: deleting a hallucinated wrench absent from O; recovering terminals, beam and panel that step one missed; and rewriting an imprecise taping into the criteria-legal knocking on. 對第一步的輸出跑 N 個獨立的 expert。論文列出它們實際做的三種修正：刪掉 O 中根本不存在、被幻覺出來的 wrench；救回第一步漏掉的 terminals、beam、panel；把不精確的 taping 改寫成 criteria 允許的 knocking on。

step 3

Summarise整合

Reconcile the N revisions and their analyses into one graph per timestamp, then link timestamps so edges can be born and die across the span. 把 N 份修正與其分析整合成每個時間戳一張圖，再把各時間戳串接起來，讓邊能在整段時間跨度上生成與消失。

\[ out_{trpl}=\mathrm{VLM}(V_t,\textit{Prompt}) \qquad (1) \] \[ out^{s_1}_t=\mathrm{VLM}(V_t,\,O,\,P,\,E,\,\textit{Prompt}) \qquad (2) \] \[ \bigl(out^{s_2}_t,\,a_i\bigr)=\mathrm{VLM}\bigl(V_t,O,P,E,\textit{Prompt},out^{s_1}_t\bigr),\qquad i\in\{1,\dots,N\} \qquad (3) \] \[ out^{s_3}_t=\mathrm{VLM}\bigl(out^{s_2}_t,\alpha,\textit{Prompt}\bigr),\qquad \{out^{s_2}_t,\alpha\}=\bigl\{out^{s_2}_t,\,a_i \mid i\in\{1,\dots,N\}\bigr\} \qquad (4) \]

Read equations (1) and (2) side by side. They differ in nothing but the presence of O, P and E. So the paper’s Table 3 — \(R@20\) climbing from 0.032 with neither, to 1.787 with O, to 20.792 with O and P, to 23.040 once examples and analysis are added — measures the value of constraining a vision-language model’s output vocabulary. It measures no architecture whatsoever. That is the real finding, and it is easy to read past. 2 · The mathematics2 · 數學

Fifteen definitions, eight propositions, every derivation shown.十五條定義、八個命題，每一步推導都寫出來。

Until 2024 these metrics circulated as verbal definitions. Lorenz et al. (CVPRW 2024, arXiv 2404.09616) showed that published implementations disagree as a direct result. What follows states each object precisely, then derives every relation between them. 直到 2024 年，這些指標都只以文字敘述流通。Lorenz 等人（CVPRW 2024，arXiv 2404.09616）證明已發表的實作因此彼此不一致。以下先精確定義每個物件，再推導它們之間的每一個關係。

2.1 · Objects2.1 · 物件

Definition 1  grounded object已定位物件

Fix a class vocabulary \(\mathcal{C}\) and a predicate vocabulary \(\mathcal{P}\). A grounded object is a pair \(v=(c,b)\) with \(c\in\mathcal{C}\) and box \(b=(x,y,w,h)\in\mathbb{R}^4\). Write \(A(b)=wh\) for its area and \(\mathrm{ctr}(b)=(x+\tfrac{w}{2},\,y+\tfrac{h}{2})\) for its centre. 固定類別詞彙 \(\mathcal{C}\) 與 predicate 詞彙 \(\mathcal{P}\)。已定位物件是一個序對 \(v=(c,b)\)，其中 \(c\in\mathcal{C}\)、box \(b=(x,y,w,h)\in\mathbb{R}^4\)。記 \(A(b)=wh\) 為面積，\(\mathrm{ctr}(b)=(x+\tfrac{w}{2},\,y+\tfrac{h}{2})\) 為中心。

Definition 2  scene graph場景圖

A scene graph over timespan \(T\) is \(G=(V,E,T)\) with \(V\) a finite set of grounded objects and \(E\subseteq V\times\mathcal{P}\times V\). An element \(t=\langle s,p,o\rangle\in E\) is a triplet. For a single image \(T\) is a point and we write \(G=(V,E)\). This is the anchor paper’s own notation. 時間跨度 \(T\) 上的場景圖為 \(G=(V,E,T)\)，其中 \(V\) 是已定位物件的有限集合，\(E\subseteq V\times\mathcal{P}\times V\)。元素 \(t=\langle s,p,o\rangle\in E\) 稱為三元組。單張影像時 \(T\) 退化為一點，記作 \(G=(V,E)\)。這是核心論文自己的記法。

Definition 3  pair projection配對投影

\(\pi:V\times\mathcal{P}\times V\to V\times V\), \(\pi(\langle s,p,o\rangle)=(s,o)\). It forgets the predicate and keeps the ordered pair. \(\pi:V\times\mathcal{P}\times V\to V\times V\)，\(\pi(\langle s,p,o\rangle)=(s,o)\)。它忘掉 predicate，保留有序配對。

2.2 · When a prediction counts2.2 · 什麼情況算命中

Definition 4  intersection over union交集比聯集

\[ \operatorname{IoU}(b,b')=\frac{A(b\cap b')}{A(b\cup b')}=\frac{A(b\cap b')}{A(b)+A(b')-A(b\cap b')}\in[0,1] \]

Proposition 1  the scale ceiling尺度上限

For any two boxes, \(\operatorname{IoU}(b,b')\le \dfrac{\min(A(b),A(b'))}{\max(A(b),A(b'))}\). A prediction twice the correct area can never exceed \(\tfrac12\), however perfectly centred. 對任意兩個 box，\(\operatorname{IoU}(b,b')\le \dfrac{\min(A(b),A(b'))}{\max(A(b),A(b'))}\)。面積為正解兩倍的預測，無論對得多準，都不可能超過 \(\tfrac12\)。

\[ A(b\cap b')\le\min\bigl(A(b),A(b')\bigr) \]
intersection lies in both交集同時屬於兩者
\[ A(b\cup b')\ge\max\bigl(A(b),A(b')\bigr) \]
union contains both聯集包含兩者
\[ \operatorname{IoU}=\frac{A(b\cap b')}{A(b\cup b')}\le\frac{\min(A(b),A(b'))}{\max(A(b),A(b'))} \]
divide the bounds兩式相除

Consequence: with \(\tau=0.5\), any box whose area ratio to the truth exceeds \(2\) is rejected before its position is even considered. Playground F3 reports this ceiling live. 推論：取 \(\tau=0.5\) 時，任何與正解面積比超過 \(2\) 的 box，還沒考慮位置就已被拒絕。Playground F3 會即時顯示這個上限。□

Definition 5  the match relation命中關係

Fix \(\tau\in(0,1]\). For a predicted triplet \(\hat t\) and a ground-truth triplet \(t\), write \(\hat t\simeq t\) when固定 \(\tau\in(0,1]\)。對預測三元組 \(\hat t\) 與 ground-truth 三元組 \(t\)，當下式成立時記 \(\hat t\simeq t\)：

\[ \underbrace{c_{\hat s}=c_s \;\wedge\; c_{\hat o}=c_o \;\wedge\; \hat p=p}_{\text{classification}} \;\wedge\; \underbrace{\operatorname{IoU}(b_{\hat s},b_s)\ge\tau \;\wedge\; \operatorname{IoU}(b_{\hat o},b_o)\ge\tau}_{\text{localization}} \]

\(\simeq\) is reflexive and symmetric but not transitive, so it is not an equivalence relation and induces no partition. Two distinct predictions may match one ground-truth triplet. \(\simeq\) 具自反性與對稱性，但不具傳遞性，因此不是等價關係，也不誘導任何分割。兩個相異的預測可能命中同一個 ground-truth 三元組。

2.3 · Ranking, constraint, and the candidate set2.3 · 排序、constraint 與候選集合

Definition 6  candidate set候選集合

A model emits scored triplets \(\hat T=\{(\hat t_i,\sigma_i)\}\), \(\sigma_i\in[0,1]\). Sort by \(\sigma\) descending and let \(X_k\) be the first \(k\). Ties are broken by a fixed rule, which is part of the specification because it is observable. 模型輸出帶分數的三元組 \(\hat T=\{(\hat t_i,\sigma_i)\}\)，\(\sigma_i\in[0,1]\)。依 \(\sigma\) 遞減排序，取前 \(k\) 個為 \(X_k\)。同分以固定規則決定先後——這屬於規格的一部分，因為它是可觀察的。

Definition 7  graph constraintgraph constraint

\(X_k\) satisfies the graph constraint when \(\pi\) restricted to \(X_k\) is injective — at most one predicate survives per ordered pair. In practice keep \(\arg\max_p\sigma(\langle s,p,o\rangle)\). Write \(X_k^{\mathrm{ng}}\) for the unconstrained candidate set, admitting up to \(m\) predicates per pair. 當 \(\pi\) 限制在 \(X_k\) 上為單射時，稱 \(X_k\) 滿足 graph constraint——每個有序配對至多留下一個 predicate。實務上保留 \(\arg\max_p\sigma(\langle s,p,o\rangle)\)。記 \(X_k^{\mathrm{ng}}\) 為不受約束的候選集合，每個配對至多容許 \(m\) 個 predicate。

Definition 8  credited matches計入的命中

Because \(\simeq\) is not an equivalence relation, “\(G\cap X_k\)” is defined as a maximum matching, not a set intersection:由於 \(\simeq\) 不是等價關係，「\(G\cap X_k\)」定義為最大匹配，而非集合交集：

\[ \mu(G,X_k)=\max\bigl\{\,\lvert M\rvert \;:\; M\subseteq X_k\times G,\ M \text{ a partial injection},\ (\hat t,t)\in M\Rightarrow \hat t\simeq t \,\bigr\} \]

Each ground-truth triplet may be credited at most once, and each prediction spends itself on at most one.每個 ground-truth 三元組至多被計入一次，每筆預測也至多消耗在一個上面。

2.4 · The recalls2.4 · 各種 recall

Definition 9  R@k, Rp@k, mR@k

Let \(N=\lvert G\rvert\), \(G^{(p)}=\{t\in G:\operatorname{pred}(t)=p\}\), \(n_p=\lvert G^{(p)}\rvert\), and \(\mathcal{P}'=\{p:n_p>0\}\) with \(C=\lvert\mathcal{P}'\rvert\). 令 \(N=\lvert G\rvert\)、\(G^{(p)}=\{t\in G:\operatorname{pred}(t)=p\}\)、\(n_p=\lvert G^{(p)}\rvert\)，以及 \(\mathcal{P}'=\{p:n_p>0\}\)、\(C=\lvert\mathcal{P}'\rvert\)。

\[ R@k=\frac{\mu(G,X_k)}{N},\qquad R_p@k=\frac{\mu\bigl(G^{(p)},X_k\bigr)}{n_p},\qquad mR@k=\frac{1}{C}\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'}R_p@k \]

Definition 10  the weighting family加權族

For \(\alpha\in[0,1]\) define \(\displaystyle w_p(\alpha)=\frac{n_p^{\alpha}}{\sum_{q\in\mathcal{P}'}n_q^{\alpha}}\) and對 \(\alpha\in[0,1]\) 定義 \(\displaystyle w_p(\alpha)=\frac{n_p^{\alpha}}{\sum_{q\in\mathcal{P}'}n_q^{\alpha}}\)，並令 \[ R_{\alpha}@k=\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'}w_p(\alpha)\,R_p@k \]

2.5 · The decomposition2.5 · 分解

Proposition 2  R@k is a frequency-weighted mean of per-predicate recallsR@k 是各 predicate recall 的頻率加權平均
\[ R@k=\frac{\mu(G,X_k)}{N} \]
Definition 9定義 9
\[ =\frac{1}{N}\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'}\mu\bigl(G^{(p)},X_k\bigr) \]
\(\{G^{(p)}\}\) partitions \(G\); a match is credited to exactly one class\(\{G^{(p)}\}\) 分割 \(G\)，每個命中只歸屬一個類別
\[ =\frac{1}{N}\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'}n_p\cdot\frac{\mu\bigl(G^{(p)},X_k\bigr)}{n_p} \]
multiply and divide by \(n_p>0\)同乘同除 \(n_p>0\)
\[ =\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'}\frac{n_p}{N}\,R_p@k \]
Definition 9 again再次套用定義 9

Since \(\sum_p n_p/N=1\) and \(\sum_p 1/C=1\), both \(R@k\) and \(mR@k\) are weighted means of the same quantities \(R_p@k\). 2.6 · Why the gap exists, exactly2.6 · 落差究竟從何而來

Proposition 3  the covariance identity共變異數恆等式

Let \(\bar n=N/C\) be the mean predicate count and let \(\operatorname{Cov}\) denote empirical covariance under the uniform distribution on \(\mathcal{P}'\). Then 令 \(\bar n=N/C\) 為 predicate 的平均數量，\(\operatorname{Cov}\) 表示在 \(\mathcal{P}'\) 上均勻分布下的經驗共變異數。則

\[ \boxed{\;R@k-mR@k=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}_{p\sim\mathcal{U}(\mathcal{P}')}\bigl(n_p,\;R_p@k\bigr)}{\bar n}\;} \]

\[ R@k-mR@k=\sum_{p}\frac{n_p}{N}R_p-\frac{1}{C}\sum_{p}R_p \]
Proposition 2 and Definition 9命題 2 與定義 9
\[ =\frac{1}{N}\sum_{p}\Bigl(n_p-\frac{N}{C}\Bigr)R_p=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{p}\bigl(n_p-\bar n\bigr)R_p \]
collect over a common denominator通分後合併
\[ =\frac{1}{N}\sum_{p}\bigl(n_p-\bar n\bigr)\bigl(R_p-\bar R\bigr) \]
\(\sum_p(n_p-\bar n)=0\), so subtracting any constant \(\bar R\) changes nothing\(\sum_p(n_p-\bar n)=0\)，故減去任何常數 \(\bar R\) 都不影響
\[ =\frac{C}{N}\cdot\frac{1}{C}\sum_{p}\bigl(n_p-\bar n\bigr)\bigl(R_p-\bar R\bigr)=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(n,R)}{\bar n} \]
definition of empirical covariance, and \(C/N=1/\bar n\)經驗共變異數的定義，且 \(C/N=1/\bar n\)

Taking \(\bar R=mR@k\) is the natural choice and makes the third line literal.□ 取 \(\bar R=mR@k\) 是自然的選擇，並讓第三行成為字面意義上的等式。□

This is the sharpest statement available about the whole field. The gap between the two headline metrics is exactly the covariance between how often a predicate occurs and how well the model does on it, divided by the mean frequency. Three corollaries follow immediately: 這是關於整個領域所能給出的最銳利的陳述。兩個主要指標之間的落差，恰好等於「某個 predicate 出現的頻率」與「模型在它上面的表現」兩者的共變異數，再除以平均頻率。三個推論立刻成立：

Condition條件Consequence結果What it describes對應什麼情形
\(\operatorname{Cov}(n,R)>0\)\(R>mR\)the model does better on common predicates — the frequency-biased regime, and every model before 2020模型在常見 predicate 上表現較好——頻率偏差區間，2020 年前的每個模型都在這裡
\(\operatorname{Cov}(n,R)=0\)\(R=mR\)performance is uncorrelated with frequency. Holds automatically when the distribution is flat, which is why the gap vanishes at Zipf exponent \(s=0\) in F7表現與頻率無關。分布為平坦時自動成立，這正是 F7 中 Zipf 指數 \(s=0\) 時落差消失的原因
\(\operatorname{Cov}(n,R)<0\)\(mR>R\)the model does better on rare predicates. The worked example below reaches this once the graph constraint is lifted模型在罕見 predicate 上表現較好。下方的手算範例在關掉 graph constraint 後就進入這個狀態

\[ \operatorname{Cov}(n,R)\approx-0.01361 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{-0.01361}{10/7}\approx-0.00952=\mathrm{ngR}-\mathrm{mNgR} \]
covariance turns negative — \(mR\) now exceeds \(R\)共變異數轉負——\(mR\) 於是超過 \(R\)

The sign flip is the whole lesson. Recovering two rare triplets moved \(R\) by \(0.200\) and \(mR\) by \(0.286\), and pushed the covariance through zero. No model changed; only the counting rule did. Playground E6 is Definition 10 as a single slider. 正負號的翻轉就是整堂課的重點。救回兩個罕見三元組讓 \(R\) 上升 \(0.200\)、\(mR\) 上升 \(0.286\)，並把共變異數推過了零點。模型毫無改變，改變的只有計數規則。Playground E6 就是把定義 10 做成一根滑桿。

2.8 · The remaining metrics2.8 · 其餘指標

Definitions 11–13  ngR@k, zR@k, PR@k

\[ \mathrm{ngR}@k=\frac{\mu\bigl(G,X_k^{\mathrm{ng}}\bigr)}{N},\qquad zR@k=\frac{\mu(G_z,X_k)}{\lvert G_z\rvert},\qquad PR@k=\frac{\bigl\lvert\pi(G)\cap\pi(X_k)\bigr\rvert}{\lvert\pi(G)\rvert} \]

where \(G_z=\{t\in G:(c_s,p,c_o)\notin\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\}\) and \(\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\) is the set of class-level triplet types seen in training. Zero-shot recall is not a new metric — it is \(R@k\) on a filtered ground truth. 其中 \(G_z=\{t\in G:(c_s,p,c_o)\notin\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\}\)，\(\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\) 是訓練時見過的類別層級三元組型態集合。Zero-shot recall 不是新指標——它就是在過濾後 ground truth 上的 \(R@k\)。

2.9 · Protocols2.9 · Protocol

Definition 14  PredCls, SGCls, SGDet

Let \(\mathcal{H}_{\text{PredCls}}\), \(\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGCls}}\), \(\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGDet}}\) be the hypothesis spaces a model may search under each protocol. PredCls supplies ground-truth boxes and labels, SGCls supplies boxes only, SGDet supplies nothing. 令 \(\mathcal{H}_{\text{PredCls}}\)、\(\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGCls}}\)、\(\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGDet}}\) 為各 protocol 下模型可搜尋的假設空間。PredCls 給 ground-truth box 與標籤，SGCls 只給 box，SGDet 什麼都不給。

\[ \lvert\mathcal{H}_{\text{PredCls}}\rvert=\lvert V\rvert^2\lvert\mathcal{P}\rvert \;\le\; \lvert\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGCls}}\rvert=\lvert V\rvert^2\lvert\mathcal{C}\rvert^2\lvert\mathcal{P}\rvert \;\le\; \lvert\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGDet}}\rvert \]

Proposition 5  the protocol chainprotocol 鏈

\[ \sup_{h\in\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGDet}}} R@k \;\le\; \sup_{h\in\mathcal{H}_{\text{SGCls}}} R@k \;\le\; \sup_{h\in\mathcal{H}_{\text{PredCls}}} R@k \]

Each protocol withholds strictly more information than the one to its right, so any hypothesis achievable under the harder protocol is achievable under the easier one by ignoring the extra input. The recall attainable is therefore non-decreasing along the chain.□ 每個 protocol 比右邊那個嚴格隱藏更多資訊，因此在較難 protocol 下可達成的任何假設，在較易的 protocol 下都可藉由忽略多餘輸入來達成。故沿此鏈可達的 recall 單調不減。□

Read the quantifier. This bounds the supremum over each hypothesis space. It does not follow that three separately trained systems will rank in that order, and a table where they do not is reporting a fact about training, not an error. The familiar ordering holds for a single system measured three times, because there the SGDet pipeline is the SGCls pipeline with detection prepended, so its predictions are a degraded copy of the same predictions. Playground E10 is built that way on purpose — it degrades localization rather than re-running detection — so there the ordering is forced by construction, and a violation on those fixtures really does mean the implementation is wrong. 請留意量詞。此式界定的是各假設空間上的上確界。由它不能推出三個分別訓練的系統必然依此順序排列；若某張表格並非如此，那反映的是訓練的事實，而不是錯誤。習見的順序成立於同一個系統被量測三次的情形，因為此時 SGDet 流程就是 SGCls 流程前面再接上偵測，其預測為同一組預測的降級副本。Playground E10 刻意依此方式建構——它是讓定位品質降級，而非重跑偵測——因此在該處順序由建構本身保證，此時若違反，確實代表實作有誤。

The standard misreading. PredCls and SGCls supply ground-truth boxes, never ground-truth pairs. The factor \(\lvert V\rvert^2\) survives in every protocol — the model still decides which of the ordered pairs are related at all. 最常見的誤讀。PredCls 與 SGCls 給的是 ground-truth box，絕不是 ground-truth 配對。因子 \(\lvert V\rvert^2\) 在每個 protocol 中都存在——模型仍須自行判斷哪些有序配對真的有關係。

2.10 · Cost2.10 · 計算成本

Proposition 6  the quadratic term平方項

A two-stage method classifying every ordered pair performs \(N_{\text{prop}}(N_{\text{prop}}-1)\lvert\mathcal{P}\rvert\) decisions, while \(\lvert E\rvert=O(N_{\text{prop}})\) in practice. At 80 proposals over GQA’s 310 predicates: \(80\cdot 79\cdot 310=1{,}958{,}800\) decisions for roughly twenty true relations — a positive rate near \(10^{-5}\). This single ratio motivates one-stage set prediction, and Pair-Net’s result that sparsifying pairs before classification is where the gain lives. Playground T1. 對每個有序配對都做分類的兩階段方法，需要 \(N_{\text{prop}}(N_{\text{prop}}-1)\lvert\mathcal{P}\rvert\) 次判斷，而實務上 \(\lvert E\rvert=O(N_{\text{prop}})\)。以 80 個提案、GQA 的 310 個 predicate 計算：\(80\cdot 79\cdot 310=1{,}958{,}800\) 次判斷，對應大約二十個真實關係——正例率約 \(10^{-5}\)。單單這個比值就構成了 one-stage 集合預測的動機，也解釋了 Pair-Net 的結論：增益來自在分類之前先稀疏化配對。見 Playground T1。

Proposition 7  greedy matching is an approximation, not an identity貪婪匹配是近似，不是恆等

Every implementation computes \(\mu(G,X_k)\) greedily in score order. Exact maximum bipartite matching costs \(O\bigl(\lvert E\rvert\sqrt{\lvert V\rvert}\bigr)\) by Hopcroft–Karp. Greedy attains the optimum whenever the bipartite graph induced by \(\simeq\) admits a unique maximum matching — in particular when \(\simeq\) is a near-bijection, which holds on natural data because two ground-truth triplets rarely share a class pair and a predicate and mutually sufficient overlap. 所有實作都以依分數排序的貪婪法計算 \(\mu(G,X_k)\)。精確的最大二分匹配用 Hopcroft–Karp 需 \(O\bigl(\lvert E\rvert\sqrt{\lvert V\rvert}\bigr)\)。當 \(\simeq\) 誘導的二分圖具有唯一最大匹配時，貪婪法即達最佳——特別是當 \(\simeq\) 接近雙射時成立，而在自然資料上這通常為真，因為兩個 ground-truth 三元組很少同時共用類別配對、predicate、以及足夠的重疊。

This is an assumption, not a theorem. It is a proof obligation the test suite discharges on adversarial fixtures — duplicated boxes at \(\operatorname{IoU}\) exactly \(\tau\), ties in \(\sigma\), and classes with a single ground-truth instance. 這是假設，不是定理。它是測試套件必須在對抗性樣本上履行的證明義務——包括 \(\operatorname{IoU}\) 恰好等於 \(\tau\) 的重複 box、\(\sigma\) 同分、以及只有單一 ground-truth 實例的類別。
