Several releases share one name數個發布版本共用同一名稱

“VG150” denotes several releases that differ in how the validation set is carved and in which images are filtered. Xu et al. (2017, §4) state a 70/30 split of 108,077 images and no counts; the canonical protocol keeps 57,723 / 5,000 / 26,446 images with at least one relation; SGG-Benchmark’s current release publishes 68,538 / 5,000 / 31,876, and an earlier release, since fixed, drew 4,844 validation images from the test pool. Papers rarely say which they used. 「VG150」指涉數個發布版本，彼此於驗證集之切取方式與影像篩選規則上有所不同。Xu 等人（2017，§4）載明 108,077 張影像依 70／30 比例切分，未列張數；標準協定保留至少含一條關係之影像 57,723／5,000／26,446 張；SGG-Benchmark 現行版本公布 68,538／5,000／31,876 張，其先前版本（已修正）曾自測試影像池抽取 4,844 張作為驗證集。論文很少載明所採用者為何。

03

The mathematics, stated properly完整寫清楚的數學

Every symbol defined before use; every metric given its formal statement每個符號先定義再使用；每個指標都給出形式化定義

Until 2024 the field’s metrics circulated as verbal definitions. Lorenz et al. (CVPRW 2024) pointed this out, gave the first rigorous statements, and showed that published implementations disagree as a result. Everything below follows their formulation. It is also the specification the app’s evaluation engine is written against. 直到 2024 年，這個領域的指標都只以文字敘述流通。Lorenz 等人（CVPRW 2024）指出了這一點，首次給出嚴謹的形式化定義，並證明已發表的實作因此彼此不一致。以下全部採用他們的表述，這也是本應用程式評測引擎的撰寫依據。

Symbols符號

\(\mathcal{C},\ \mathcal{P}\)
object class and predicate vocabularies. On VG150, \(|\mathcal{C}|=150\) and \(|\mathcal{P}|=50\).物件類別詞彙與 predicate 詞彙。在 VG150 上，\(|\mathcal{C}|=150\)、\(|\mathcal{P}|=50\)。
\(v=(c,b)\)
a grounded object: class \(c\in\mathcal{C}\) and box \(b\in\mathbb{R}^4\).一個已定位的物件：類別 \(c\in\mathcal{C}\) 與 box \(b\in\mathbb{R}^4\)。
\(t=\langle s,p,o\rangle\)
a triplet: subject \(s\), predicate \(p\in\mathcal{P}\), object \(o\).一個三元組：subject \(s\)、predicate \(p\in\mathcal{P}\)、object \(o\)。
\(G=(V,E,T)\)
the ground-truth scene graph over timespan \(T\); \(E\subseteq V\times\mathcal{P}\times V\). For a single image \(T\) collapses to a point. This is the anchor paper’s own notation.時間跨度 \(T\) 上的 ground-truth 場景圖；\(E\subseteq V\times\mathcal{P}\times V\)。單張影像時 \(T\) 退化為一個點。這是核心論文自己使用的記法。
\(\hat{T}=\{(\hat t_i,\sigma_i)\}\)
the model’s scored predictions, \(\sigma_i\in[0,1]\).模型輸出的帶分數預測，\(\sigma_i\in[0,1]\)。
\(X_k\)
the first \(k\) predictions after sorting \(\hat T\) by \(\sigma\) descending.將 \(\hat T\) 依 \(\sigma\) 遞減排序後的前 \(k\) 筆。
\(\tau\)
the localization threshold, \(\tau=0.5\) by convention.定位門檻，慣例為 \(\tau=0.5\)。

1 · When does a prediction count?1 · 什麼情況算預測正確？

Intersection over union measures box agreement — the shared area divided by the total area covered: Intersection over union 衡量兩個 box 的吻合程度——交集面積除以聯集面積：

\[ \operatorname{IoU}(b,b') \;=\; \frac{\lvert b\cap b'\rvert}{\lvert b\cup b'\rvert} \;\in\;[0,1] \]

A predicted triplet \(\hat t\) matches a ground-truth triplet \(t\), written \(\hat t\simeq t\), when all five conditions hold at once: 當以下五個條件同時成立時，預測三元組 \(\hat t\) 命中 ground-truth 三元組 \(t\)，記作 \(\hat t\simeq t\)：

\[ \hat t \simeq t \iff \underbrace{c_{\hat s}=c_s \;\wedge\; c_{\hat o}=c_o \;\wedge\; \hat p = p}_{\text{classification}} \;\wedge\; \underbrace{\operatorname{IoU}(b_{\hat s},b_s)\ge\tau \;\wedge\; \operatorname{IoU}(b_{\hat o},b_o)\ge\tau}_{\text{localization}} \]

Why the app draws four colours, not two.為什麼介面用四種顏色而不是兩種。 The conjunction splits cleanly into a classification half and a localization half. A prediction can fail either one independently — and the diff view colours those two failures differently, because they are the exact distinction the three evaluation protocols exist to isolate. 這個合取式乾淨地分成「分類」與「定位」兩半，預測可以只在其中一半失敗。差異檢視把這兩種失敗畫成不同顏色，因為這正是三種評測 protocol 存在的目的所在。

A subtlety worth a slide.值得用一張投影片講的細節。 \(\simeq\) is not an equivalence relation — it is not transitive, and two distinct predictions can both match the same ground-truth triplet. So “\(\cap\)” below is not set intersection; it is the size of a maximum partial injection \(M\subseteq X_k\times G\) with \((\hat t,t)\in M\Rightarrow \hat t\simeq t\). Every implementation approximates \(M\) greedily in score order. Exact maximum bipartite matching would cost \(O(|E|\sqrt{|V|})\) by Hopcroft–Karp; greedy coincides with the optimum whenever \(\simeq\) is near-bijective, which it almost always is. That coincidence is an assumption, not a theorem — and it is a proof obligation the engine’s test suite has to discharge on adversarial fixtures. \(\simeq\) 不是等價關係——它不具傳遞性，而且兩個不同的預測可能命中同一個 ground-truth 三元組。因此下面的「\(\cap\)」並不是集合交集，而是最大部分單射 \(M\subseteq X_k\times G\)（滿足 \((\hat t,t)\in M\Rightarrow \hat t\simeq t\)）的大小。所有實作都用依分數排序的貪婪法近似 \(M\)。精確的最大二分匹配用 Hopcroft–Karp 需要 \(O(|E|\sqrt{|V|})\)；當 \(\simeq\) 接近雙射時貪婪法與最佳解一致，而實務上幾乎總是如此。但這個一致性是假設而非定理——它是評測引擎的測試套件必須在對抗性樣本上證明的義務。

2 · The graph constraint2 · Graph constraint

Let \(\pi(\langle s,p,o\rangle)=(s,o)\) project a triplet onto its ordered pair. The graph constraint is the requirement that \(\pi\) be injective on the candidate set: 令 \(\pi(\langle s,p,o\rangle)=(s,o)\) 把三元組投影到它的有序配對。Graph constraint 就是要求 \(\pi\) 在候選集合上為單射：

\[ \forall\, t,t'\in X_k:\quad t\neq t' \;\Longrightarrow\; \pi(t)\neq\pi(t') \]

In practice: for each ordered pair keep only \(\arg\max_{p}\sigma(\langle s,p,o\rangle)\). Dropping the requirement gives the no-constraint variant, written \(\mathrm{ngR}@k\). Action Genome adds a third, semi-constraint mode. The three are not comparable, and the difference routinely runs to ten or twenty points. 實務做法：每個有序配對只保留 \(\arg\max_{p}\sigma(\langle s,p,o\rangle)\)。拿掉這個要求就得到 no-constraint 版本，記作 \(\mathrm{ngR}@k\)。Action Genome 另外加了第三種 semi-constraint 模式。三者彼此不可比較，差距動輒十到二十個百分點。

3 · The two recalls3 · 兩種 recall

\[ R@k \;=\; \frac{\lvert G\cap X_k\rvert}{\lvert G\rvert} \]

Write \(G^{(p)}=\{t\in G:\operatorname{pred}(t)=p\}\) for the ground-truth triplets carrying predicate \(p\), and \(\mathcal{P}'=\{p:G^{(p)}\neq\varnothing\}\) for the predicates actually present. Then 令 \(G^{(p)}=\{t\in G:\operatorname{pred}(t)=p\}\) 表示帶有 predicate \(p\) 的 ground-truth 三元組，\(\mathcal{P}'=\{p:G^{(p)}\neq\varnothing\}\) 表示實際出現過的 predicate。則

\[ mR@k \;=\; \frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal{P}'\rvert}\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'} \frac{\lvert G^{(p)}\cap X_k^{(p)}\rvert}{\lvert G^{(p)}\rvert} \;=\; \frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal{P}'\rvert}\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'} R_p@k \]

4 · The identity that explains the whole field4 · 解釋整個領域的那個恆等式

Let \(n_p=\lvert G^{(p)}\rvert\). Partitioning \(G\) by predicate and summing gives, immediately, 令 \(n_p=\lvert G^{(p)}\rvert\)。把 \(G\) 依 predicate 分割後求和，立刻得到

\[ R@k \;=\; \sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'} \frac{n_p}{\lvert G\rvert}\, R_p@k \qquad\text{versus}\qquad mR@k \;=\; \sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}'} \frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal{P}'\rvert}\, R_p@k \]

The two metrics average the same per-predicate recalls \(R_p@k\) under different weights. \(R@k\) weights by ground-truth frequency \(n_p/\lvert G\rvert\); \(mR@k\) weights every predicate equally at \(1/\lvert\mathcal{P}'\rvert\). Nothing else differs. 兩個指標平均的是同一組各 predicate 的 recall \(R_p@k\)，只是權重不同。\(R@k\) 用 ground-truth 頻率 \(n_p/\lvert G\rvert\) 加權；\(mR@k\) 則給每個 predicate 相同的權重 \(1/\lvert\mathcal{P}'\rvert\)。除此之外毫無差別。

Two consequences follow without further argument. First, on VG150 a handful of predicates — on, has, wearing, of, in, near — carry most of the mass, so a model that does well on those alone maximizes \(R@k\) while \(mR@k\) stays near zero. That is precisely the frequency baseline. Second, \(mR@k>R@k\) is perfectly possible: it happens exactly when the model does better on rare predicates than on common ones. The prototype above reaches that state once the constraint is lifted. 由此立刻得到兩個結論。其一，在 VG150 上少數幾個 predicate（on、has、wearing、of、in、near）佔了絕大部分質量，因此只要在這幾個上表現好，就能把 \(R@k\) 推高，而 \(mR@k\) 仍接近零——這正是 frequency baseline 的作法。其二，\(mR@k>R@k\) 完全可能發生：恰好當模型在罕見 predicate 上的表現優於常見 predicate 時。上方的原型一旦關掉 constraint 就會進入這個狀態。

Under the constraint, \(\texttt{above}\) and \(\texttt{picking up}\) are suppressed because a higher-scoring predicate already occupies their pair, and \(\texttt{wearing}\) fails on \(\operatorname{IoU}=0.43<\tau\). Applying both formulas: 開啟 constraint 時，\(\texttt{above}\) 與 \(\texttt{picking up}\) 被壓掉，因為分數更高的 predicate 已經佔住了它們的配對；\(\texttt{wearing}\) 則因 \(\operatorname{IoU}=0.43<\tau\) 而失敗。代入兩個公式：

\[ \begin{aligned} R@20 &= \tfrac{3}{10}\!\cdot\!\tfrac{2}{3} + \tfrac{2}{10}\!\cdot\!1 + \tfrac{1}{10}\!\cdot\!1 + \tfrac{1}{10}\!\cdot\!1 + 0 + 0 + 0 &&= 0.600\\[4pt] mR@20 &= \tfrac{1}{7}\left(\tfrac{2}{3} + 1 + 1 + 1 + 0 + 0 + 0\right) &&\approx 0.524 \end{aligned} \]

Lift the constraint. The model is unchanged; only the candidate set grows: 關掉 constraint。模型完全沒變，只是候選集合變大：

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{ngR}@20 &= \tfrac{3}{10}\!\cdot\!\tfrac{2}{3} + \tfrac{2}{10}\!\cdot\!1 + \tfrac{1}{10}\!\cdot\!1 \cdot 4 + 0 &&= 0.800\\[4pt] \mathrm{mNgR}@20 &= \tfrac{1}{7}\left(\tfrac{2}{3} + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 0\right) &&\approx 0.810 \end{aligned} \]

Read the second line again.請再讀一次第二行。 \(mR\) overtakes \(R\), \(0.810 > 0.800\), because the two recovered triplets are both singleton classes and the unweighted mean pays them seven times what the frequency-weighted mean pays. The same two predictions move \(R\) by \(0.200\) and \(mR\) by \(0.286\). No model changed. Only the counting rule did. \(mR\) 超過了 \(R\)，\(0.810 > 0.800\)，因為救回來的那兩個三元組都屬於只有一個實例的類別，而未加權平均給它們的權重是頻率加權平均的七倍。同樣兩筆預測讓 \(R\) 上升 \(0.200\)、讓 \(mR\) 上升 \(0.286\)。模型沒有任何改變，改變的只有計數規則。

6 · The remaining metrics, and one invariant6 · 其餘指標，以及一個不變量

Zero-shot recall is not a separate metric. Let \(\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\) be the set of class-level triplet types \((c_s,p,c_o)\) seen in training; then \(zR@k\) is simply \(R@k\) evaluated on the filtered ground truth \(G_z=\{t\in G:(c_s,p,c_o)\notin\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\}\). Pair recall drops the predicate entirely, \(PR@k=\lvert\pi(G)\cap\pi(X_k)\rvert/\lvert\pi(G)\rvert\), and bounds \(R@k\le PR@k\) — which is how Pair-Net identified pair detection, not relation classification, as the real bottleneck in panoptic SGG. Zero-shot recall 不是獨立的指標。令 \(\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\) 為訓練時見過的類別層級三元組型態 \((c_s,p,c_o)\) 的集合；則 \(zR@k\) 就只是在過濾後的 ground truth \(G_z=\{t\in G:(c_s,p,c_o)\notin\mathcal{T}_{\text{train}}\}\) 上計算的 \(R@k\)。Pair recall 則完全捨棄 predicate，\(PR@k=\lvert\pi(G)\cap\pi(X_k)\rvert/\lvert\pi(G)\rvert\)，並給出上界 \(R@k\le PR@k\)——Pair-Net 正是靠這個界指出 panoptic SGG 真正的瓶頸在配對偵測，而不在關係分類。

The three protocols differ only in what is given. PredCls supplies \(V\) complete with labels; SGCls supplies boxes but withholds labels; SGDet supplies nothing. Each strictly enlarges the search space, which yields an invariant the test suite asserts on every fixture: 三種 protocol 的差別只在於「給了什麼」。PredCls 提供完整含標籤的 \(V\)；SGCls 提供 box 但不給標籤；SGDet 什麼都不給。每一種都嚴格擴大了搜尋空間，因此得到一個測試套件會在每個樣本上檢驗的不變量：

\[ R_{\text{SGDet}}@k \;\le\; R_{\text{SGCls}}@k \;\le\; R_{\text{PredCls}}@k \]

The misreading this catches.這條式子能擋下的誤解。 PredCls and SGCls hand the model ground-truth boxes — never ground-truth pairs. The model still has to decide which of the \(\lvert V\rvert^2\) ordered pairs are related at all. This is the most common misreading in the field, and it is a designed quiz item. PredCls 與 SGCls 給的是 ground-truth box，絕不是 ground-truth 配對。模型仍必須自行判斷 \(\lvert V\rvert^2\) 個有序配對中哪些真的存在關係。這是這個領域最常見的誤讀，也是刻意設計的測驗題目。

7 · The anchor paper’s own equations7 · 核心論文自己的方程式

IndVisSGG replaces the learned relation head with a prompted vision-language model \(\mathrm{VLM}(\cdot)\). Let \(V_t\) be the visual input at timestamp \(t\), and let the Triplets Extraction Criteria comprise a predefined object set \(O\), predicate set \(P\), and worked examples \(E\). The naive call is IndVisSGG 用一個被提示的 vision-language model \(\mathrm{VLM}(\cdot)\) 取代了學習出來的關係預測頭。令 \(V_t\) 為時間戳 \(t\) 的視覺輸入，Triplets Extraction Criteria 由預先定義的物件集合 \(O\)、predicate 集合 \(P\) 與範例 \(E\) 組成。最樸素的呼叫是

\[ out_{trpl} \;=\; \mathrm{VLM}(V_t,\ \textit{Prompt}) \tag{1} \]

which produces unconstrained free-text relations. Supplying the criteria constrains the output vocabulary:這會產生不受約束的自由文字關係。提供 criteria 之後就能約束輸出詞彙：

\[ out^{s_1}_t \;=\; \mathrm{VLM}(V_t,\ O,\ P,\ E,\ \textit{Prompt}) \tag{2} \]

Step two runs \(N\) independent experts, each auditing step one’s output and returning both a revision and an analysis \(a_i\):第二步跑 \(N\) 個獨立的 expert，各自審查第一步的輸出，回傳修正結果與一份分析 \(a_i\)：

\[ \bigl(out^{s_2}_t,\ a_i\bigr) \;=\; \mathrm{VLM}\bigl(V_t,\ O,\ P,\ E,\ \textit{Prompt},\ out^{s_1}_t\bigr),\qquad i\in\{1,\dots,N\} \tag{3} \]

Step three reconciles them, with \(\alpha\) collecting the experts’ analyses:第三步進行整合，\(\alpha\) 匯集所有 expert 的分析：

\[ out^{s_3}_t \;=\; \mathrm{VLM}\bigl(out^{s_2}_t,\ \alpha,\ \textit{Prompt}\bigr), \qquad \{out^{s_2}_t,\alpha\}=\bigl\{out^{s_2}_t,\ a_i \;\big|\; i\in\{1,\dots,N\}\bigr\} \tag{4} \]

What the ablations are really measuring.消融實驗真正在量什麼。 Equations (1) and (2) differ only in whether \(O\), \(P\), and \(E\) are present — so the paper’s Table 3, where \(R@20\) climbs from \(0.032\) to \(23.040\) as those arguments are added, is measuring the value of constraining a VLM’s output vocabulary, not the value of any architecture. Table 3 is a five-row factorial, and the effect is superadditive: \(O\) alone scores \(1.787\), \(P\) alone \(2.079\), and both together \(20.792\) — an order of magnitude beyond either. Constraining one end of the triplet is nearly worthless; constraining both is transformative. The paper does not remark on this. Equation (3) is a sampling procedure over \(N\); Table 4 reports \(N=3\) as optimal, and \(N=3\) is genuinely not dominated — \(N=5\) leads at \(k=20\) but loses on \(mR@50\) (\(24.383\) against \(25.480\)) and on \(R@100\) (\(30.020\) against \(30.142\)). No theory accompanies any of it. Lab L5 lets students vary \(N\) and \(O,P,E\) and watch both tables reassemble. 方程式 (1) 與 (2) 的唯一差別，就是 \(O\)、\(P\)、\(E\) 在不在——因此論文 Table 3 中 \(R@20\) 從 \(0.032\) 一路爬到 \(23.040\)，量的是「約束 VLM 輸出詞彙」的價值，而不是任何架構的價值。Table 3 是五列的因子設計，且效果具超加成性：單給 \(O\) 為 \(1.787\)，單給 \(P\) 為 \(2.079\)，兩者同時給則為 \(20.792\)——比任一者高出一個數量級。只約束三元組的一端幾乎沒有作用，兩端同時約束才具決定性。論文本身並未論及此點。方程式 (3) 則是對 \(N\) 的一種抽樣程序；Table 4 回報 \(N=3\) 最佳，而 \(N=3\) 確實未被全面超越——\(N=5\) 在 \(k=20\) 領先，但 \(mR@50\) 落後（\(24.383\) 對 \(25.480\)）、\(R@100\) 亦落後（\(30.020\) 對 \(30.142\)）。這一切都沒有理論支撐。Lab L5 讓學生自行改動 \(N\) 與 \(O,P,E\)，看著這兩張表自己重新長出來。

The cache-first rule stands, on corrected reasoning. The ARM64 objection applies to the teaching machine only — the development machine is AMD64 with a CUDA device. Where should the repository live? — settled程式碼要放在哪裡？ — 已定案

Inside the WekaExt repository at scene-graph-studio/, not as a repository of its own. It moved there from the teaching repository on 2026-09-19. 02

Which VLM backs L5? — Claude, opt-inL5 要接哪個 VLM？ — Claude，選用

The offline transcript player stays the default and remains the only provider any must-ship feature may require. A live Claude path is configured through .env, never committed, and reported by the health endpoint without ever reporting the key. Two implementations is the minimum that proves VLMProvider is a protocol rather than one class with an interface bolted on. 離線逐字稿播放仍為預設，且是任何必交付功能唯一可依賴的供應者。即時的 Claude 路徑透過 .env 設定、絕不納入版控，並由 health endpoint 回報是否已設定，但永遠不回報金鑰內容。實作兩個供應者是證明 VLMProvider 確為協定（而非事後加上介面的單一類別）的最低要求。

03

Where do the two-stage predictions come from? — labelled reconstructionsTwo-stage 的預測結果從哪來？ — 標示為重建

RelTR and EGTR are produced here and committed as measured. Neural Motifs and PSGFormer ship as reconstructed files — scripted to reproduce the behaviour their papers document, carrying a required note saying so, and rendered in a visibly different style. Fidelity is a field in the data model, not a caveat in a caption, so nothing can quietly present a reconstruction as a model’s output. RelTR 與 EGTR 在本專案產生，以 measured（實測）納入版控。Neural Motifs 與 PSGFormer 則以 reconstructed（重建）形式出貨——以腳本重現其論文所記載的行為，必須附上說明欄位，並以明顯不同的樣式呈現。來源層級是資料模型中的欄位，而非圖說裡的但書，因此任何地方都無法悄悄把重建結果當成模型輸出呈現。

04

Is fifteen modules the right size? — kept, with the corpus cut instead十五個模組份量對嗎？ — 維持，改為縮減論文卡

All fifteen modules stay. What was cut is the expensive part: every reported figure must be read off a named table, so the paper corpus now carries 35 cards in two tiers — roughly twelve scored methods with full verified numbers, and the rest with venue, DOI, core idea, predecessor and the defect each fixes, but no numbers at all. That serves the goal of placing any paper on the taxonomy completely, and takes roughly two hundred verified figures down to seventy. There is no unverified tier: a number nobody has checked against its table is not carried. 十五個模組全數保留。被縮減的是成本高的部分：每一個引用數值都必須從指名的表格上讀出，因此論文卡現為 35 張、分兩個層級——約十二篇實際被評分的方法帶完整且已查證的數值，其餘僅列出場次、DOI、核心想法、前作，以及各自修正了前作的哪一個缺陷，完全不帶數值。這已足以完整支撐「把任一篇論文定位到分類體系上」的教學目標，並使需查證的數值從約兩百個降為七十個。不設「未查證」層級：沒有對照表格查證過的數字，一律不收錄。
