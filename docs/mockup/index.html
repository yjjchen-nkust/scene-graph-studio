直覺Intuition
R@K 問的是「模型前 K 個猜測裡，抓回了多少應該抓回的關係」。它沒有任何 precision 項：多猜不會受罰。 R@K asks how much of what should have been found was found inside the model's top K guesses. There is no precision term anywhere: guessing more never costs anything.
R@k = |G ∩ X_k| / |G|     k′ ≥ k ⟹ R@k′ ≥ R@k
實例演算Worked example
GT = 10 · matched = 9 → R@20 = 0.900 per-class: on 9/9 = 1.000 · rare 0/1 = 0.000 mR@20 = (1.000 + 0.000) / 2 = 0.500
意涵Implications
只出現一次的類別，在 mR 裡與出現九次的類別等重。整個偏差問題就是一個共變異數的正負號：R@k − mR@k = Cov(n, R)/n̄。 A class with one instance weighs exactly as much as one with nine. The whole bias problem is the sign of a single covariance: R@k − mR@k = Cov(n, R)/n̄.

論文卡（A 層）A paper card, tier A

MOTIFS-TDE · CVPR 2020 · doi:10.1109/CVPR42600.2020.00377
以因果推論移除訓練分布造成的偏差Unbiased SGG from biased training

核心想法。在推論時從預測中扣除「只看標籤共現就能得到的部分」，留下真正由畫面支持的部分。 Core idea. Subtract, at inference time, the part of the prediction obtainable from label co-occurrence alone, leaving what the pixels actually support.

前作與其缺陷。Neural Motifs —— 它的頻率先驗強到讓一個完全不看畫面的預測器在 mR@100 上勝過它。 Predecessor and its defect. Neural Motifs, whose frequency prior is strong enough that a predictor reading no pixels at all beats it on mR@100.

不會宣稱的事What this will not claim

錨定論文所述的 ISG 資料集並未公開發布 —— 2026 年 9 月查證。本教材依已發表的表格講授它，標示為「需來函索取」，並提醒 Hugging Face 上的 ISG-Bench 是同名不同物。替代品是以相同方法、由授權開放的工業影像自建的 mini-ISG，明確標示為本專案的教學集，不是作者的 ISG。 The ISG dataset described in the anchor paper has no public release — verified September 2026. This material teaches it from the published tables, labels it request-only, and warns that ISG-Bench on Hugging Face is a name collision. In its place a mini-ISG is built by the same method from openly licensed industrial frames, labelled unambiguously as our teaching set and not the authors'.
